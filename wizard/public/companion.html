This is the work window

Every outside page the wizard opens — GitHub, Cloudflare, your calendar, Brevo — appears here, one page at a time, while the instructions stay put in the wizard's own window. Read there, act here.

Try moving between the two windows now. On the way back, the first click may only bring the wizard's window forward, and a second click is needed for the button itself — the browser's doing, not yours.

Closing this window does no harm: the next link opens it afresh. If it hides behind other windows, the wizard's “Show the work window” button brings it back.
