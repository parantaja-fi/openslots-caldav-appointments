Set up your booking calendar

Setting up needs a GitHub account and a Cloudflare account, both free — use existing ones or create new ones; the wizard guides either way. Nothing is installed on your computer and you need no server of your own: your own calendar is the only storage.

Your customer → Booking pageon GitHub → Booking APIon Cloudflare → Your calendarstays where it is

This wizard keeps track of where you are and highlights the next needed action — read ahead and come back freely. If you close the page midflight, the wizard may lose some detail: done steps stay done, but a few values are asked again. It re-checks the world every twenty seconds; the Check again button asks at once. SETUP.md holds the why behind every step. At the “Store the configuration” step, below, your passwords and API keys go, encrypted, straight to your own copy of the project on GitHub — nowhere else.

  1. Two free accounts — GitHub hosts your page, Cloudflare runs the API.
  2. Your own copy of the project on GitHub — one click.
    Everything else — the Worker's address, your page's URL — the wizard discovers from the deploy itself.
  3. Where your OPEN hours live and where bookings are written.
  4. A token that lets the deploy publish the booking API.
  5. The Brevo key and the address your confirmations come from.
  6. How your OPEN blocks are cut into bookable slots.
  7. One token paste — everything lands encrypted in your fork.

    The knobs are remembered by this browser; passwords, keys and tokens stay in this tab's memory until you write them to your fork — close the tab and you will paste them again.

  8. One switch so GitHub may publish your booking page.
  9. One button — GitHub builds and ships both halves.
  10. The API checks its own wiring and reports back.
  11. Records at your domain so confirmation emails get delivered.
  12. An OPEN event in your calendar: the time customers may book.
  13. Book, get the email, cancel — the whole loop, once.

All green — your booking calendar is live.

Living with it: bookings land in your calendar as ordinary events, and cancelling one from its email link frees the slot. Your open hours are the OPEN events in the availability calendar — edit those, and the page follows. To change a setting later, come back here: adjust the form, paste a fresh token, store and deploy again.

The setup token has done its job — revoke it now. Day-to-day booking needs no standing access to your GitHub account.
