Set up your booking calendar

You are about to get a booking calendar of your own: a public booking page, a small program guarding it, and your existing calendar as the only place anything is stored. Two free services run it — nothing to install, no server to keep. This page walks the whole journey and watches each step land.

Your customer → Booking pageon GitHub → Booking APIon Cloudflare → Your calendarstays where it is

SETUP.md is the long-form reference when you want the why behind a step. Your passwords and keys go, encrypted, straight to your own copy of the project on GitHub — nowhere else — and are forgotten when the tab closes.

Everything else — the Worker's address, your page's URL — the wizard discovers from the deploy itself.

Configuration

Everything your deployment needs, checked as you type. The knobs are remembered by this browser; passwords, keys and tokens stay in this tab's memory until you write them to your fork — close the tab and you will paste them again.

    All green — your booking calendar is live.

    Living with it: bookings land in your calendar as ordinary events, and cancelling one from its email link frees the slot. Your open hours are the OPEN events in the availability calendar — edit those, and the page follows. To change a setting later, come back here: adjust the form, paste a fresh token, write and deploy again.

    The setup token has done its job — revoke it now. Day-to-day booking needs no standing access to your GitHub account.
