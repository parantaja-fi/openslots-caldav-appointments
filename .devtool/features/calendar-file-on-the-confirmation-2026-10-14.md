# Calendar file on the confirmation

> Written 2026-10-14, from that day's competitor comparison: Tymeslot
> and calrs attach one.

## Why

Today the customer gets the appointment into their own calendar only by
typing it. An `.ics` attachment on the confirmation makes that one
action. It needs no state: everything in it is at hand when the
confirmation is built.

## What

- **The customer's confirmation only.** The practitioner's notice needs
  none; the event is already in their calendar.
- **`METHOD:PUBLISH`**: a copy to add, not an invitation. `REQUEST`
  would make it an iTIP invitation, which needs an `ORGANIZER`, offers
  accept and decline, and sends replies to the organiser's address.
- **The VEVENT**:
  - `UID` derived from the booking uid with a fixed suffix, not equal to
    it. A customer whose calendar lives on the store's server, above all
    the practitioner testing with their own address, must not collide
    with or overwrite the booking itself. Derived rather than random, so
    that a later `METHOD:CANCEL`, should a cancellation email ever
    exist, can name it without state.
  - `DTSTART` and `DTEND` in UTC, as in the store; `DTSTAMP`.
  - `SUMMARY` "Appointment with {`SENDER_NAME`}" when the operator set
    one, else "Appointment" — not the configuration's "Bookings"
    fallback.
  - No `ATTENDEE` or `ORGANIZER`.
- **Generated by hand**, like the store's VEVENT (`ARCHITECTURE.md` §2),
  reusing `buildVEvent`'s escaping and folding; an optional `METHOD`
  line there is the smallest change. No dependency.
- **Transport.** Brevo's `attachment: [{ name, content }]`, `content`
  base64, `name` e.g. `appointment.ics`; `ics` is on Brevo's list of
  allowed extensions (<https://developers.brevo.com/reference/sendtransacemail>,
  read 2026-10-14). Encode the UTF-8 bytes, not the string: names may be
  non-ASCII, and `btoa` takes Latin-1. `Message` gains an optional
  attachment, and `sendEmail()` passes it through, so the seam (§6)
  stays one function.

## Decide: the cancellation link in the event

Convenient: cancel from the calendar entry. But the event syncs to the
customer's calendar provider, and shows to anyone they share that
calendar with; the bearer token would travel with it until slot start,
where §5.2 keeps it in a URL fragment precisely to limit where it
lands. Recommendation: leave it out, and put "To cancel, use the link in
your confirmation email." in `DESCRIPTION` instead.

## Measure, do not assume

Client handling of a `PUBLISH` attachment varies, and the Brevo call
sets no MIME type: how the sent part is typed, and whether a client
sees a `method`, has to be observed. Try Gmail (web and Android),
Outlook.com, Apple Mail (macOS and iOS) and Thunderbird. For each,
record in `LESSONS.md` whether it offers to add the event, in how many
actions, and at the right local time. If a major client ignores the
attachment, revisit the method with that evidence rather than by
reasoning — the M2 `<c:expand>` lesson.

## Tests

- Unit, `email.test.ts` against the stubbed transport: the confirmation
  carries one attachment; it decodes to a VCALENDAR with
  `METHOD:PUBLISH`, the derived `UID`, the slot's `DTSTART` and `DTEND`,
  and no token; the practitioner's notice carries none.
- `npm run roundtrip`: the received confirmation has an `.ics`
  attachment with a calendar content type, which proves that Brevo
  passes it through. Confirm testmail.app's attachment fields when
  writing it.

## Documents

`ARCHITECTURE.md` §6 says the confirmation carries the attachment.
`PRIVACY.md` needs checking but no change is expected: the attachment
repeats what the message body already says.
